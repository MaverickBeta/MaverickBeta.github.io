Research · Duke Kunshan University

Visual Grounding & Multimodal Reasoning

Research assistant: Jun – Sep 2026 · Advisor: Prof. Kaizhu Huang · Follow-up experiments ongoing

Research Question

How do vision-language models use image evidence when generating answers, and why do they sometimes introduce absent details or misread information that is present?

My Work

  • Analyzed failures in plane-geometry reasoning and optical character recognition, focusing on small models and error patterns also seen in larger models.
  • Built whiteboard handwriting recognition prototypes that convert visual content into structured files, addressing local deployment, error detection, and recovery.
  • Used attention maps as an initial view of how image-derived information reaches answer tokens.

Ongoing Causal Analysis

Attention weights alone cannot establish which visual information shaped an output. Inspired by causal analyses of visual information pathways, I am adapting this approach to the observed errors and testing how language priors affect the use of image evidence. These experiments are ongoing, with the goal of understanding and improving visual grounding.

Research Direction

This work connects my earlier studies of Transformer mechanisms and visual representations with my interest in more reliable multimodal reasoning.

研究项目 · 昆山杜克大学

视觉依据与多模态推理

研究助理：2026年6月 – 9月 · 导师：Kaizhu Huang 教授 · 后续实验仍在进行

研究问题

视觉语言模型生成答案时如何使用图像证据？它们为何会添加图像中不存在的细节，或误读已经存在的信息？

我的工作

  • 分析平面几何推理与光学字符识别中的失败，重点关注小模型，以及大模型中同样出现的错误模式。
  • 构建白板手写识别与结构化文件转换原型，处理本地部署、错误检测与恢复。
  • 利用注意力图，初步观察图像信息如何到达答案 token。

正在进行的因果分析

仅凭注意力权重，无法确定哪些视觉信息实际影响了输出。我正在借鉴视觉信息路径的因果分析方法，将其用于观察到的错误，并检验语言先验如何影响图像证据的使用。目前实验仍在进行，目标是理解并改进模型对视觉依据的使用。

研究方向

这项工作将我此前对 Transformer 机制与视觉表征的研究，连接到更可靠的多模态推理这一目标。
