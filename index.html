In-context learning & model alignment

上下文学习与模型对齐

Lawrence Carin · Duke University

Lawrence Carin 教授 · 杜克大学

I evaluate Transformer architectures inspired by gradient-descent theories of in-context learning, using ARC, MMLU, and ablation studies. My work on SAE-based detoxification examines the limits of feature-level interventions and has motivated a shift toward fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for alignment.

通过 ARC、MMLU 与消融实验，我研究受上下文学习梯度下降理论启发的 Transformer 架构。同时，我探索 SAE 去毒化与特征级干预的局限，并将研究延伸至监督微调和强化学习对齐。

Visual grounding in vision-language models

视觉语言模型的视觉依据

Kaizhu Huang · Duke Kunshan University

Kaizhu Huang 教授 · 昆山杜克大学

I study OCR and geometry errors to understand how vision-language models use image evidence. Building on whiteboard recognition prototypes, my ongoing follow-up experiments examine how visual information and language priors shape generated answers.

从 OCR 与几何任务中的错误出发，我研究视觉语言模型如何使用图像证据。在白板手写识别原型的基础上，后续实验正在检验视觉信息与语言先验如何共同影响模型的答案。

Visual representations & energy-based models

视觉表征与能量模型

Kaizhu Huang · Duke Kunshan University

Kaizhu Huang 教授 · 昆山杜克大学

I investigated SAE channel expansion for robust CNN classification, finding that representation structure limited its usefulness under our training setup. I also explored generation initialization to partially improve background diversity in energy-based image generation.

我研究通过 SAE 扩展 CNN 通道能否帮助鲁棒分类，并发现表征结构限制了该方法在本实验设置下的作用。此外，我探索生成初始化方法，以部分改善能量模型生成图像的背景多样性。
