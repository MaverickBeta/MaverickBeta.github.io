In-Context Learning & Language Model Alignment

Sep 2025 – Present

Student Research Assistant · Advisor: Prof. Lawrence Carin, Duke University

  • Evaluated modified Transformer architectures motivated by gradient-descent theories of in-context learning against vanilla baselines on ARC and MMLU, studying models at 353M and 1.3B parameters.
  • Observed comparable baseline performance and a substantial performance drop when ablating the ICL-related computation, examining how theoretical predictions carry over to language models.
  • Explored SAE features for detoxification in Gemma-2-2B and modified Transformers. Sensitivity to training data and hyperparameters, dead features, and inconsistent gains motivated a shift toward supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for alignment.

Visual Representations & Energy-Based Models

Dec 2025 – May 2026

Student Research Assistant · Advisor: Prof. Kaizhu Huang, Duke Kunshan University

  • Investigated whether channel expansion with SAEs could disentangle CNN features relevant to robust classification in energy-based models.
  • Under our training setup, CNN activations were already sparse; SAE encoding and decoding did not yield useful additional feature decomposition, and their performance cost outweighed robustness gains.
  • Observed limited background diversity in class-conditioned generation. Starting generation from an unrelated label partially alleviated this effect.

上下文学习与语言模型对齐

2025年9月 – 至今

学生研究助理 · 导师：Lawrence Carin 教授，杜克大学

  • 在 ARC、MMLU 等基准上，将基于上下文学习梯度下降理论的改进型 Transformer 与标准基线比较，研究规模包括 353M 和 1.3B 参数模型。
  • 观察到改进架构与基线表现相近，而消融上下文学习相关计算会导致性能明显下降，据此考察理论预测在语言模型规模下的表现。
  • 在 Gemma-2-2B 和改进型 Transformer 中探索 SAE 特征与去毒化干预。训练数据和超参数敏感性、失活特征及不稳定收益，促使研究转向监督微调和强化学习对齐。

视觉表征与能量模型

2025年12月 – 2026年5月

学生研究助理 · 导师：Kaizhu Huang 教授，昆山杜克大学

  • 研究通过 SAE 扩展 CNN 通道维度，能否解耦与能量模型鲁棒分类相关的特征。
  • 在本实验设置下，CNN 激活已经较为稀疏，SAE 编解码未带来有效的额外特征分解，其性能代价也超过了鲁棒性收益。
  • 观察到按类别生成图像时背景多样性受限；从无关类别开始生成，可以部分缓解这一现象。
