Research · Duke University

SAE Detoxification & Model Alignment

Sep 2025 – Present · Advisor: Prof. Lawrence Carin, Duke University
View on GitHub

Research Question

Can token-level hidden representations be decomposed into interpretable features and used for targeted interventions on harmful language generation?

My Work

  • Explored SAEs to interpret activation patterns in Gemma-2-2B and modified Transformer architectures.
  • Identified features associated with harmful generations and intervened on them to steer the models toward less harmful outputs.
  • Examined whether the learned features were sufficiently stable and useful for reliable intervention.

Findings & Limitations

The approach was highly sensitive to hyperparameters and to the activation data used for training, and dead features were common. Training on a sufficiently representative activation distribution was computationally expensive. Sparsity alone did not guarantee interpretable or useful features, and SAE-based detoxification did not consistently outperform alternative methods.

Subsequent Direction

After discussing these limitations, we shifted our focus to supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to align model outputs with our intended principles.

SAE 去毒化与模型对齐

2025年9月 – 至今 · 导师：Lawrence Carin 教授，杜克大学
在 GitHub 上查看

研究问题

能否将 token 级隐藏表征分解为可解释特征，并利用这些特征对有害文本生成进行定向干预？

我的工作

  • 使用 SAE 探索 Gemma-2-2B 与改进型 Transformer 的激活模式。
  • 识别与有害生成相关的特征，并通过干预这些特征，引导模型减少有害输出。
  • 考察学得的特征是否足够稳定，以及是否能够支持可靠干预。

发现与局限

该方法对超参数和训练激活数据高度敏感，失活特征也较常见。在足够有代表性的激活分布上训练 SAE，计算成本较高。稀疏目标本身不保证特征可解释或适合干预，基于 SAE 的去毒化也没有持续优于其他方法。

后续方向

在讨论这些局限后，我们将重点转向监督微调和强化学习，以使模型输出符合预期原则。
