Research · Duke University

In-Context Learning in Modified Transformers

Sep 2025 – Present · Advisor: Prof. Lawrence Carin, Duke University

Research Question

How do Transformers learn from context, and do predictions from gradient-descent theories of in-context learning persist at the scale of language models?

My Work

  • Studied the theoretical basis of modified Transformer architectures previously developed in the group at 353M and 1.3B parameters.
  • Evaluated these architectures against vanilla Transformers in in-context learning settings on benchmarks including ARC and MMLU.
  • Ablated the ICL-related computation to examine its contribution to model performance.

Observations & Interpretation

The modified architectures performed comparably to vanilla baselines, while ablating the ICL-related computation caused a substantial performance drop. These observations informed my investigation of the hypothesis that inference can resemble gradient descent on a proxy objective; they do not, by themselves, establish a general account of Transformer inference.

Connection to Model Alignment

This work led me to ask whether modified architectures produce token-level representations that are easier to interpret and use for targeted interventions. I subsequently explored SAE-based detoxification and the practical limitations of feature-level steering.

研究项目 · 杜克大学

改进型 Transformer 的上下文学习机制

2025年9月 – 至今 · 导师：Lawrence Carin 教授，杜克大学

研究问题

Transformer 如何从上下文中学习？上下文学习梯度下降理论的预测，能否在语言模型规模下成立？

我的工作

  • 学习课题组此前开发的改进型 Transformer 的理论基础，研究规模包括 353M 和 1.3B 参数模型。
  • 在 ARC、MMLU 等基准的上下文学习设置中，将改进架构与标准 Transformer 比较。
  • 消融上下文学习相关计算，考察其对模型表现的贡献。

观察与解释

改进架构与标准基线表现相近，而消融上下文学习相关计算后，模型性能明显下降。这些观察帮助我研究“推理是否类似于对代理目标进行梯度下降”的假设，但其本身不足以构成对 Transformer 推理的一般性证明。

与模型对齐的联系

这项工作促使我进一步思考：改进架构是否能产生更容易解释、也更适合定向干预的 token 级表征？随后，我探索了基于 SAE 的去毒化，以及特征级引导在实践中的局限。
